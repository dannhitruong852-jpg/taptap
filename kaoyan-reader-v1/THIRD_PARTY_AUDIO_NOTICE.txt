2002 C-mode v3 audio reference notice

The 2002 content-selected cast uses anonymous-speaker reference excerpts from EARS: Expressive Anechoic Recordings of Speech, by Richter et al. (Interspeech 2024).
Source: https://github.com/facebookresearch/ears_dataset
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0)
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

Reference excerpts are selected, trimmed, peak-normalized and resampled before being used as conditioning audio for synthetic Chatterbox TTS speech. The resulting study audio is for noncommercial educational use. No EARS speaker, author, institution, or dataset maintainer endorses this project.

The 15-slot actor registry is an editorial role system. It does not claim that all 15 voices have been materialized. For 2002, only the content-selected actors needed by the six units and their explicit dialogue roles are materialized.
